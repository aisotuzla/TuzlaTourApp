# PROJECT PITCH: Tuzla Smart Destination Ecosystem
**A Unified Application Aligning with Interreg Europe "Smart Tour" Pillars**

**The Problem:** Traditional tourism in European cities is fragmented. Tourists must juggle multiple apps for finding parking, calling taxis, booking hotels, navigating cultural heritage, and making local payments. This disconnects the tourist from the local economy and creates digital friction.

**The Solution:** The Tuzla Smart App is an all-in-one digital ecosystem that transforms Tuzla into a premier European Smart Destination. It wraps urban mobility, local commerce, and immersive cultural exploration into a single cohesive wallet and map experience.

### Core Features & EU Pillar Alignment
1. **Digital Empowerment (The Super-App):** Users can call a taxi, pay for parking, book accommodations, and order food directly from local SMEs with delivery to their door. 
2. **Sustainability (Eco-Mobility):** Gamified walking tours (MapQuest) guide users to cultural sites, reducing vehicle congestion. Smart parking features prevent driving aimlessly.
3. **Culture & Creativity (AR & Web3):** Tourists use augmented reality to scan hidden QR codes at monuments (like King Tvrtko). Completing quests unlocks exclusive City/National Team NFTs. These digital souvenirs hold real-world utility—acting as discount passes for food and drinks during local events (e.g., Summer World Cup viewings).
4. **Accessibility:** A fully multilingual, highly accessible interface utilizing offline-capable maps (Jawg Maps/MapLibre) ensuring tourists are never lost, even without data roaming.
5. **Holistic Economy (Digital Wallet):** A built-in digital wallet keeps tourist spending within the local economy and provides actionable, anonymized analytics to city managers for agile decision-making.

**Vision for Tuzla:** 
By adopting this platform, Tuzla skips a generation of fragmented municipal apps, arriving immediately at the forefront of European tourism tech. The app fulfils the EU's Transition Pathway for Tourism (TPT) requirements, laying the groundwork for Tuzla to be a model Smart Destination for the entire Balkan region.
