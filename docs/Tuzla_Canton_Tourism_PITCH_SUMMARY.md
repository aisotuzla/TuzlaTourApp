## WHY TUZLA? 5 PILLARS

### 1. 🧂 SALT — Heritage Unlike Anywhere Else
The Pannonian Salt Lakes are unique in the world. No other European city offers urban open-air salt-water bathing. Salt therapy, salt heritage museums, and the story of a city built on salt mines create an unreplicable brand identity.

### 2. 💰 VALUE — 60% Cheaper Than Croatia
Tuzla offers EU-quality experiences at developing-world prices. A dental implant costs €400–700 (vs. €800–1,400 in Hungary). A 3-course dinner for two costs €15–25 (vs. €30–50 in Croatia). Budget travelers stretch their money 2–3x further.

### 3. 🏔️ NATURE — Mountains, Lakes, Rivers
Protected Konjuh mountain landscape (1,327m), Lake Modrac (17 km²), thermal springs, 6 major rivers — all within a 45-minute drive. Hiking, kayaking, cycling, paragliding.

### 4. 🎭 CULTURE — Where Empires Collided
Ottoman mosques next to Austro-Hungarian facades. Medieval tombstones (stećci) connected to UNESCO World Heritage. Gradačac Castle. Srebrenik Fortress. Multi-ethnic festivals spanning Muslim, Orthodox, and Catholic traditions.

### 5. 📱 TECHNOLOGY — AI-First Tourism
The Tuzla Virtual Tour Guide app is operational: 3D interactive map, GPS navigation, AR historical guides, gamified QR code quests, AI chatbot powered by Google Gemini. This is the most technologically advanced tourism product in the Western Balkans.
