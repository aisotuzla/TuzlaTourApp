🗺️ 100% Offline Parking Map Strategy

To ensure the parking map functions strictly offline and uses no cellular/Wi-Fi data:

1. ZOOM LOCK We will restrict the map's offline zoom level strictly to 16 & 17 (matching the pre-downloaded tiles). This prevents requests for missing zoom tiles, eliminating 404 network errors.
2. OFFLINE FAIL-SAFE A fallback stylesheet logic will catch MapLibre loading failures. If the geocoding service is offline or unreachable, the map immediately swaps to the local offline style JSON.
3. LOW-ACCURACY GPS FALLBACK If high-accuracy GPS times out or fails (common under heavy cover), the map will fall back to lower-accuracy cell tower tracking and cache the last known coordinate.
