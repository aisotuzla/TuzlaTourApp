## 📋 COMMON STRUCTURE OF PROJECT PROPOSALS IN BOSNIA

Based on the research, project proposals in Bosnia and Herzegovina typically follow this structure:

### 1. Cover Page
- Project title
- Applicant organization
- Contact information
- Date
- Project duration
- Total budget

### 2. Executive Summary (1-2 pages)
- Project overview
- Objectives
- Expected results
- Total budget
- Funding request

### 3. Project Background
- **Context:** Current situation, problems, needs
- **Justification:** Why this project is necessary
- **Stakeholders:** Who will benefit, who is involved
- **Previous work:** Related projects, lessons learned

### 4. Project Objectives
- **Overall objective:** Long-term impact
- **Specific objectives:** SMART (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
- **Expected results:** Quantifiable outcomes

### 5. Project Activities
- **Work plan:** Detailed description of activities
- **Methodology:** How activities will be implemented
- **Timeline:** Schedule, milestones
- **Responsibilities:** Who will do what

### 6. Project Budget
- **Cost breakdown:** Itemized list of all costs
- **Funding sources:** Own contribution, requested funding, other sources
- **Justification:** Why each cost is necessary

### 7. Monitoring and Evaluation
- **Indicators:** How success will be measured
- **Reporting:** Frequency and format of reports
- **Evaluation:** How the project will be evaluated

### 8. Sustainability
- **Financial sustainability:** How the project will continue after funding ends
- **Institutional sustainability:** Capacity of implementing organization
- **Social sustainability:** Long-term benefits for the community

### 9. Annexes
- **CVs:** Of key project staff
- **Letters of support:** From partners, stakeholders
- **Financial statements:** Of applicant organization
- **Other relevant documents:** Permits, licenses, etc.

## 💡 KEY INSIGHTS FROM RESEARCH

### 1. Tourism is a Priority Sector
- Bosnia and Herzegovina has **identified tourism as a key economic sector**
- **Multiple international organizations** (UNDP, USAID, EU) are investing in tourism development
- **Public-private partnerships** are common and encouraged

### 2. Digital Transformation is Growing
- **Increasing adoption** of digital technologies in tourism
- **Mobile apps** are becoming essential for tourist destinations
- **Gamification** and **AR/VR** are emerging trends

### 3. Funding Opportunities Exist
- **EU funds** (IPA, Horizon Europe)
- **UNDP** (Sustainable development projects)
- **USAID** (Economic growth initiatives)
- **Bilateral cooperation** (Bulgaria, Italy, etc.)
- **Local government budgets**

### 4. Partnership Models Work
- **Public-private partnerships** are successful in tourism
- **Co-financing models** (50/50 or 70/30) are common
- **Multi-stakeholder approaches** (government, private sector, NGOs) are preferred

### 5. Sustainability is Key
- **Projects must demonstrate long-term impact**
- **Financial sustainability** is a major evaluation criterion
- **Local ownership** and **capacity building** are important
