## 🎮 HOW THE QUEST GAME WORKS?

### 1. User Journey

```
1. User downloads Tuzla Tour App (PWA - no installation needed)
2. Starts Quest Game (4 phases of discovering Tuzla)
3. To complete each phase, user must:
   - Visit specific locations (GPS validation within 5-15m)
   - Scan QR codes at locations
   - Solve puzzles related to the location
4. After visiting, user receives:
   - Digital rewards (NFT, trophies)
   - Exclusive discount coupons
   - Points for leaderboard
```

### 2. Your Role as a Partner

**A. Set Up QR Code**
- We provide you with a **personalized QR code**
- Place it in a visible location at your business
- Users scan to confirm their visit

**B. AR POI (Augmented Reality Point of Interest)**
- Users see a **3D marker** above your location through the camera
- Marker contains information about your business
- Enables precise navigation

**C. Coupons and Rewards**
- Define **exclusive discounts** for app users
- Coupons are automatically generated after visit
- You can track **how many coupons were used**
