## 2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet) when you visit a website or use a web application. They allow the website to:

- Remember your preferences and settings
- Recognize your device on subsequent visits
- Improve site functionality and performance
- Analyze how users interact with the site
- Serve personalized content and advertisements

---

## 3. Types of Cookies We Use

We categorize cookies based on their purpose and duration:

### 3.1 By Purpose

#### A. Strictly Necessary Cookies
- **Purpose:** Essential for the App to function properly
- **Consent Required:** No (exempt under GDPR Article 6(1)(f))
- **Can Be Disabled:** No (would break core functionality)

**Examples:**
- Session cookies for maintaining user state
- Authentication cookies (if applicable)
- Security cookies
- Load balancing cookies

#### B. Performance/Analytical Cookies
- **Purpose:** Help us understand how users interact with the App
- **Consent Required:** Yes (opt-in under GDPR)
- **Can Be Disabled:** Yes

**Examples:**
- Google Analytics cookies (`_ga`, `_gid`, `_gat`)
- Vercel Analytics cookies
- Speed Insights cookies

#### C. Functional Cookies
- **Purpose:** Remember user preferences and settings
- **Consent Required:** Yes (opt-in under GDPR)
- **Can Be Disabled:** Yes

**Examples:**
- Language preference cookies
- Theme preference cookies (if dark mode is added)
- Location preference cookies

#### D. Marketing/Advertising Cookies
- **Purpose:** Track user behavior for advertising purposes
- **Consent Required:** Yes (opt-in under GDPR)
- **Can Be Disabled:** Yes

**Note:** Tuzla Tour App **currently does NOT use** marketing/advertising cookies. This section is included for future reference.

### 3.2 By Duration

#### Session Cookies
- **Duration:** Until you close your browser
- **Purpose:** Maintain user session state
- **Automatically Deleted:** Yes

#### Persistent Cookies
- **Duration:** Varies (from days to years)
- **Purpose:** Remember preferences across sessions
- **Automatically Deleted:** No (must be manually cleared or expire)

## 6. How to Control Cookies

### 6.1 Browser Settings

You can control cookies through your browser settings:

#### Chrome
1. Go to **Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data**
2. Select your preferred option

#### Firefox
1. Go to **Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data**
2. Select your preferred option

#### Safari
1. Go to **Preferences > Privacy**
2. Select your preferred cookie blocking option

#### Edge
1. Go to **Settings > Privacy, search, and services > Cookies and site permissions**
2. Select your preferred option

### 6.2 Mobile Devices

#### Android (Chrome)
1. Open Chrome app
2. Tap **More > Settings > Site settings > Cookies**
3. Enable/Disable cookies

#### iOS (Safari)
1. Open Settings app
2. Scroll down and tap **Safari**
3. Under **Privacy & Security**, configure your cookie settings

### 6.3 Opt-Out Tools

#### Google Analytics
- **Browser Add-on:** [Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout/)
- **Effect:** Prevents Google Analytics from tracking your visits

#### Industry Opt-Out
- **NAI Opt-Out:** [Network Advertising Initiative](https://optout.networkadvertising.org/)
- **DAA Opt-Out:** [Digital Advertising Alliance](https://optout.aboutads.info/)
- **YourAdChoices:** [YourAdChoices by DAA](https://www.youradchoices.com/)
