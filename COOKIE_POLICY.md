# Cookie & Storage Notice — Tuzla Tour Guide

**Last reviewed:** 16 November 2026  
**Applies to:** Tuzla Tour Guide website, PWA and supported Capacitor builds.

## What we store

Tuzla Tour Guide uses browser local storage or Capacitor Preferences for essential app settings, including the visitor's language, local app preferences and whether this short privacy notice has been dismissed. These values are not advertising cookies.

The app does not intentionally use Google Tag Manager, Google Analytics 4, advertising pixels or marketing cookies.

## Analytics and hosting

The app keeps Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights enabled. Vercel Web Analytics is designed to operate without cookies; Speed Insights collects performance measurements. These services may process technical request and device information as described in [Vercel's Privacy Policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy).

The website is hosted on Vercel. Map tiles, fonts, audio and other external resources may be delivered by third-party providers. Those providers may receive technical connection information when your device requests their resources. Their handling of data is governed by their respective policies.

## Location and notifications

Location is used when you choose location-based features such as maps, navigation and proximity alerts. The app checks location on the device for its current geofence alerts.

Firebase Cloud Messaging and device-local notifications are used only for notification features. The app should ask for notification permission only after you choose **Enable notifications**. A device push token may be stored locally and sent to Firebase to route notifications. You can disable notification permission in your browser or device settings.

## Managing your data

You can clear the app's site data in your browser, or clear/reset app storage or uninstall the mobile app. This also removes locally stored preferences and may reset your local quest progress.

This notice is informational; it is not a substitute for the full [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md). The project operator should review actual production requests and third-party services before public launch.
