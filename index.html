Discover Tuzla & Europe's Only Urban Salt Lakes

Tuzla is the third-largest city in Bosnia and Herzegovina, celebrated for over 6,000 years of continuous salt extraction. Its premier attraction, Panonika (Pannonian Lakes), is Europe's only urban saltwater lake resort, featuring three artificial lakes with salt water, continiously filtering during the day, with mineral salinity of 30–35 g/L matching seawater, cascading waterfalls, and an authentic reconstructed Neolithic stilt village.

Airport Transit (TZL to City Center)

Tuzla International Airport (TZL) is connected to downtown Tuzla via Bus Line 34 (fares 2.50–5.50 KM, cash only, ~35–50 min). Official taxis operate outside arrivals with standard fares between 30 and 40 BAM (€15–20).

Top Attractions & Highlights

  • Pannonian Lakes (Panonika): Therapeutic saltwater complex in city center.
  • Trg Slobode (Freedom Square): Largest town square in Bosnia with baroque fountain.
  • Soni Trg (Salt Square): Historic 2,500-year salt well archaeological display.
  • King Tvrtko I Monument: Central park statue and Charter of Kulin Ban (1189).
  • Kapija Memorial: Sacred historic monument to peace and youth in the pedestrian core.
