

Bosnia and Herzegovina National Football Team
Team Overview
Nickname: "Zmajevi" (The Dragons) or "Zlatni Ljiljani" (Golden Lilies)
FIFA Ranking: 71st (as of January 2026)
Captain: Edin Džeko (their all-time leading scorer with 72 goals in 146 caps)
Head Coach: Sergej Barbarez
Home Stadium: Bilino Polje Stadium in Zenica
World Cup History
Bosnia and Herzegovina has qualified for two FIFA World Cups in their history:

2014 Brazil: Their debut World Cup appearance, where they finished third in Group F with one win (3-1 vs Iran) and two losses (to Argentina and Nigeria)
2026 (Canada/USA/Mexico): Their second-ever World Cup qualification

UEFA Playoff Path - The Dramatic Qualification
Path A Semifinal - March 26, 2026:

Wales 0-1 Bosnia and Herzegovina in Cardiff
Armin Gigović scored the decisive goal in the 14th minute
This victory ended Bosnia's playoff curse - they had never won a playoff match in seven previous attempts (3 draws, 4 losses)
Path A Final - March 31, 2026:

Bosnia and Herzegovina 1-1 Italy (4-1 on penalties) in Zenica
Italy took an early lead through Moise Kean (15th minute)
Alessandro Bastoni received a red card in the 41st minute, reducing Italy to 10 men
Haris Tabaković scored a dramatic equalizer in the 79th minute
After a scoreless extra time, Bosnia won 4-1 in the penalty shootout
This victory ended Italy's World Cup qualification drought - they have now missed three consecutive World Cups (2018, 2022, 2026)
This was a massive upset, as Italy is a four-time World Cup champion and Bosnia had historically struggled in playoff matches.

Significance of Qualification
This qualification is historic for Bosnia and Herzegovina because:

It's only their second World Cup appearance
They broke their playoff jinx (never winning in seven previous attempts)
They defeated a major European power (Italy) to qualify
They'll face a host nation (Canada) in their opening match
They achieved this under coach Sergej Barbarez, a former player who took over in April 2024
The Dragons will look to improve on their 2014 performance, where they showed promise but were eliminated in the group stage despite a respectable showing against eventual finalists Argentina.
